# D4 - Make state_changed match its emission

`signal state_changed(goal, action)` is emitted with a single combined
"Goal Action" string; Godot fills the second arg with null, so the declaration
lies. Lowest-risk fix: declare `state_changed(text)`.

Detail and evidence: `../IMPROVEMENT_PLAN.md` (Track D).
